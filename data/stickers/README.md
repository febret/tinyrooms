These test stickers are generated using the SBURB session simulator
(http://www.farragofiction.com/SBURBSim/oc_generator.html).

They are the preset choices offered by the Sticker Designer activity. The
"Design your own" mode composes its own original SVG parts (see
`activities/sticker-designer/parts/`) and the rendered results are stored per
account under `TRSERVER_CUSTOM_STICKERS_PATH` (`/.local/stickers` by default),
not in this directory.
